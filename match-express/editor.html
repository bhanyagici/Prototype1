Keyboard shortcuts

Road: drag the points; with the road tool, click the road to add one (it lands inside the target zone), right-click a point to delete it. Ramps: each ramp is shaped by its nodes. Drag the front node (diamond) along the road: it snaps to the road (green ring) and becomes the boarding point; dropped away from the road it turns red and the ramp gets a warning. Blockers: pick the tunnel, lock box or count box tool and drag across the cells of a ramp. Queue: drag buses to reorder, Shift-click to select up to three and press L to connect them. Exports are frozen copies: later edits never change an exported file or the game page.
