Keyboard shortcuts

Road: drag the points; with the road tool, click the road to add one (it lands inside the target zone). The exit tunnel is the road's end: drag it anywhere in the target zone. Ramps: each ramp is shaped by its nodes. Drag the front node (diamond) along the road: it snaps to the road (green ring) and becomes the boarding point; dropped away from the road it turns red and the ramp gets a warning. Blockers: pick the tunnel, lock box or count box tool and drag across the cells of a ramp. Queue: 2–5 lanes (− / +); Add bus picks lane, colour, seats and hidden; drag buses within and between lanes; Shift-click two or three touching buses (also across neighbouring lanes) and press L to connect them. Everywhere: right-click for a menu; copy, paste, duplicate, delete and the arrow keys work on whatever is selected. Exports are frozen copies: later edits never change an exported file or the game page.
